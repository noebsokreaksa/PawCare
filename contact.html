FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I get a response?

Our team typically replies within one business day. For urgent medical concerns about your pet, please contact your local vet or an emergency animal hospital directly.

Do you offer emergency pet advice?

PawCare is an educational resource, not an emergency service. If your pet is in distress, contact an emergency vet clinic right away rather than waiting on a message reply.

Can I request a specific topic to be covered?

Absolutely — use the "Subject" field above to let us know what you'd like to see, and our writers will consider it for an upcoming guide or article.
